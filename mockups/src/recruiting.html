Prep guide

Two rounds. Here is exactly what is in each.

Round 1 · Behavioral

01

Know your résumé

Every line is fair game. Be ready to go three questions deep on anything you wrote.

02

Why this fund

"I want to learn" is not an answer. Name a position we hold and what you'd ask about it.

03

A market view

One idea, held with conviction, that you can defend against pushback. It does not have to be right.

04

Questions for us

Ask about the process, the curriculum, or a sector team. Come with three.

Round 2 · Technical

01

The three statements

How they link. Walk depreciation of $10 through all three, after tax.

02

DCF mechanics

Free cash flow, WACC, terminal value. Know which assumption the answer is most sensitive to.

03

Relative valuation

EV/EBITDA versus P/E, and when each one lies. Build a comp set out loud.

04

A stock pitch

Thesis, catalyst, risk, and what would make you wrong. Four minutes.
