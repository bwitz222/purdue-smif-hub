Methodology

Returns are time-weighted and net of transaction costs. The benchmark is the S&P 500 total return index, sourced monthly. Risk statistics use trailing monthly observations with the 3-month Treasury bill as the risk-free rate. Drawdown is measured peak-to-trough on month-end values. Past performance does not indicate future results. Figures shown on this mockup are illustrative.
