Our history

Founded 2009

The fund began with a mandate from the Daniels School to let students manage real university capital under faculty supervision. Seventeen years and roughly six hundred analysts later, the mandate is unchanged.

Selection

15 to 25 from 100+

Each cycle draws more than a hundred applications for fifteen to twenty-five seats. Selection is by interview across two rounds, one behavioral and one technical, run by the executive board.

Oversight

Faculty-supervised

Two Daniels School faculty advisors sit over the fund. They do not pick positions — students vote on every one — but the investment policy statement and the risk limits are agreed with them.

The process

Four steps from idea to position

Each step produces something written. That is what makes it teachable — and auditable.

  1. 01

    Idea generation

    Screens, industry reading, and the macro view from the Fixed Income & Macro desk. Ideas start at the sector team, not the board.

    Produces: a one-page idea memo

  2. 02

    Deep research

    Financial statement analysis, a DCF, a comparable-company set, and the bear case written out in full. The sector head reviews before anything reaches the room.

    Produces: a model and a written thesis

  3. 03

    Pitch & debate

    Defended live in front of the full fund. Questions come from every sector team, not just your own. Rejected ideas come back with feedback that sharpens the next pitch.

    Produces: a recorded pitch and Q&A

  4. 04

    Vote & monitor

    The fund votes. Accepted ideas are sized by the risk desk against the IPS limits and then monitored every semester — including a written post-mortem when a thesis breaks.

    Produces: a position, and a review each semester

Philosophy

Bottom-up, thesis-driven, and slow to trade.

We buy businesses, not tickers. A position needs a thesis we can state in two sentences, a catalyst we can date, and a stated condition that would tell us we are wrong.

The index sleeve carries market return so the sector teams can take deliberate active risk around it. Turnover is low by design: if the thesis has not changed, neither has the position.

Investment policy statement

The rules we operate under

Long-term capital appreciation, measured against the S&P 500 total return index over rolling three-year periods. Education is a co-equal objective: the fund exists to train analysts, and process quality is assessed alongside return.

US-listed common equity and exchange-traded funds with a minimum market capitalization and daily liquidity threshold. No derivatives, no leverage, no short positions, no private placements.

Single-name active positions are capped as a percentage of invested capital, and sector weights are bounded relative to the benchmark. The index sleeve is exempt from the single-name cap — it is the diversification, not a bet.

Portfolio beta is monitored against a target band around 1.0. The risk desk publishes annualized volatility, Sharpe, and a 95% one-day VaR each cycle. A position whose beta materially exceeds the book average is size-capped regardless of conviction.

Every buy, trim, and exit is voted on by the full fund after a live pitch. The board does not override a vote. Faculty advisors approve the policy, not the positions.

Holdings and performance are published continuously on this site. A semester report goes out each May and December, and an annual review each January. Losing positions receive the same written treatment as winners.
